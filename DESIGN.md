# DESIGN.md: Instant Server landing

Direction for the marketing page. antislop is the filter on top of this file.

## Read

Reading this as: infrastructure landing page for Indonesian developers and small
agencies buying their first cloud box, in a gold-on-ink editorial style,
dial ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 1.

## Identity

- Product: cloud servers, S3-compatible object storage, WhatsApp gateway API.
  Sold in US Dollars, monthly, no setup fee.
- Audience: technical buyers in Indonesia. They compare specs and prices, they
  do not read marketing prose.
- Personality: quiet, precise, expensive-looking. A private bank, not a startup.

## Palette

Two core colors plus neutrals. No third hue anywhere.

- Ink `#08080c` (background), `#f3f2ef` (text), `#a6a6b4` (secondary text).
- Gold `#d8b26a`, light gold `#f0d9a8`. Gold means brand or action, never both at
  once on one screen: the primary CTA is the only filled gold element in view.

## Typography

- Manrope for everything: it holds up at 12px in spec chips and at 62px in the
  headline, and its open apertures survive the dark background.
- Instrument Serif italic for exactly one word per page (the promise word in the
  H1). It marks the sentence a buyer remembers, and nothing else uses it.

## Composition

- Left-aligned, never centered. Sections alternate between a two-column split
  (heading left, content right) and a full-width stack, so the rhythm changes
  without changing the type scale.
- One gold hairline rule above each section heading is the identity motif.
- Whitespace separates sections; there are no section borders or alternating
  background bands.
- Cards only for products, where the buyer compares price against spec.

## Motion

MOTION 1. Hover and focus states only. No scroll reveals, no parallax, no
autoplay video.

## Copy

Indonesian first, English second, same meaning in both. Prices and specs come
from the billing panel. No uptime number, no customer logo, no testimonial, no
datacenter claim that we cannot verify: the servers run in Hetzner Singapore and
the page says so.
